    About me

    Hi, I'm Joey, a creative based in Lipa City, Batangas. On weekdays I help brands with marketing, design, and content. The rest of the time, I'm usually behind a camera.

    I like photos that feel like you were there: someone mid-laugh, a quiet look across a room, light falling through a window. A lot of what I shoot ends up in black and white. The rest leans warm.

    If you'd like photos of your people, your event, or your pet, I'd love to hear about it.
